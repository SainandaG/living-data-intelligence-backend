# 🚀 Functional Guide: Living Data Intelligence

This guide walks you through the core features and workflows of the platform, from initial connection to advanced AI-driven analysis.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Connecting Data
The platform is designed to be "pluggable" with existing relational and non-relational databases.

- **Connection Hub**: Use the desktop-style modal to enter your credentials (Postgres, MySQL, Neon).
- **Auto-Discovery**: Upon connection, the **Neural Core** immediately begins scanning your tables, calculating row counts, and identifying foreign key relationships to build the initial 3D graph.

---

## 2. Navigating the 3D Universe
The main dashboard is a spatial representation of your data environment.

- **Galaxy View**: Your standard view where tables are "Planets" and relationships are "Gravitational Bonds".
- **Drill-Down**: Click any node to "Enter" the table. You will see a 3D representation of live records flowing from the source.
- **Camera Controls**:
  - **Orbit**: Rotate the view to see the cluster density.
  - **Zoom**: Focus on specific sub-graphs or "Satellite" tables.
  - **Follow**: Pin a node to keep it in focus while other parts of the graph evolve.

---

## 3. The Intelligence Panels
The UI provides specialized "Lenses" to focus on different analytical dimensions.

- **Health Lens**: Visualizes system vitals. Nodes glow brighter if they have high traffic and dim if they are "Dormant".
- **Anomaly Lens**: Highlights nodes with unusual patterns (e.g., GPS geofence breaches in the WEZU domain).
- **Latent Lens**: Switches to a semantic manifold view, grouping tables by their "Meaning" rather than just their foreign keys.

---

## 4. AI Analyst Chat & Voice Control
Collaborate with the system using natural language.

- **Analyst Chat**: Ask questions like *"Which tables are most impacted by the batteries update?"* or *"Show me the relationship between stations and GPS logs."*
- **Voice Commands**: The platform supports voice-activated navigation.
  - *"Zoom into batteries"*
  - *"Show anomalies"*
  - *"Switch to Latent View"*
- **Status HUD**: Monitor the current "Thought Loop" of the AI agents. You can see when an agent is "Patrolling" or "Deep-Scanning" a table.

---

## 5. The Time Machine (Evolution Mode)
The platform tracks the state of your data over time.

- **Timeline Player**: Play back historical data snapshots to see how relationships evolved.
- **Snapshot Selection**: Choose specific points in time to compare graph topologies and health scores.

---

## 6. Collaborative Analysis (Collaborative Mode)
Analysis is not a solo effort.

- **Collaborative Cursors**: See where your colleagues are looking in the 3D graph in real-time.
- **Shareable Deep-Links**: Generate a URL that captures your exact camera position, selected nodes, and active filters to share with your team.
