# Project Roadmap

The Living Data Intelligence Platform is continuously evolving. Below is our roadmap detailing the journey from initial prototype to enterprise-ready production release.

> **Interactive Version**: Navigate to the **Roadmap** tab in the application for the full interactive timeline visualization.

---

## Timeline Overview

| Phase | Period | Status |
|-------|--------|--------|
| Foundation & Intelligence | Nov 2025 – May 2026 | ✅ Completed |
| Advanced Intelligence | Jun 2026 – Nov 2026 | 🚀 In Progress |
| Enterprise Scale & Ecosystem | Dec 2026 – 2028 | 📋 Planned |

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & Intelligence (Nov 2025 – May 2026) ✅

- ✅ **Graph Creation Engine** — 3D schema visualization with Three.js force-directed graphs
- ✅ **Dual Clustering System** — Heuristic + NetworkX graph-theory clustering
- ✅ **Pattern Recognition** — Automated relationship discovery & anomaly detection
- ✅ **Analytics Pipeline** — Real-time TPS monitoring, SHAP explainability, ML insights
- ✅ **AI Intelligent Automation** — Gemini-powered NL→SQL, smart query generation
- ✅ **Production v1.0** — First production deployment with full CI/CD pipeline

## Phase 2: Advanced Intelligence (Jun 2026 – Nov 2026) 🚀

- ✅ **Work on Data (ML Subsystem)** — Classification, Regression, Time Series Forecasting, PDF reports
- ✅ **Enterprise Security Hardening** — JWT auth, Firebase Auth, RBAC, MFA/TOTP, rate limiting, audit logs
- ✅ **Secret Management** — All credentials via `.env`, never hardcoded
- 🔄 **Latent Space Projections** — Global & table-level latent space visualization
- 🔄 **Multiplayer Collaboration** — Real-time cursors, shared perspectives, war rooms
- 📋 **Advanced Lineage Views** — Perspective lineage with analyst/business modes
- 📋 **Production v2.0** — Second major production release

## Phase 3: Enterprise Scale & Ecosystem (Dec 2026 – 2028) 📋

- 📋 **Cloud Native Deployment** — Kubernetes Helm charts, Terraform provisioning
- 📋 **Distributed Tracing** — Full OpenTelemetry integration across microservices
- 📋 **Plugin Architecture** — Community-built custom visualizers & ML predictors
- 📋 **Streaming Data Integrations** — Native Kafka, Kinesis, RabbitMQ support
- 📋 **Global Multi-Tenant** — Multi-region deployment with tenant isolation
- 📋 **Production v5.0** — Enterprise-ready fifth production release
