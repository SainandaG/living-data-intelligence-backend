# Explainable AI (XAI)

The Living Data Intelligence Platform integrates Explainable AI (XAI) to ensure that every machine learning prediction and analysis is transparent, interpretable, and actionable.

## What is Explainable AI?
Explainable AI refers to methods and techniques in the application of artificial intelligence such that the results of the solution can be understood by humans.

## SHAP Integration

Our advanced ML subsystem uses **SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations)** to break down predictions:

### Input
- **Dataset features**: E.g., customer demographics, transaction histories, schema metadata.
- **Model predictions**: The raw outputs of Random Forest or Gradient Boosting models.

### Output
- **Feature Importance Charts**: Visual graphs indicating which factors drove the prediction (e.g., "Age" and "Income" accounted for 80% of the variance).
- **Natural Language Insights**: Textual summaries automatically generated by Gemini, explaining the SHAP values in plain English (e.g., "The model predicted a high likelihood of churn primarily due to a 30% drop in login frequency over the last month").

## Why it Matters
By providing transparent outputs, users can trust the model's decisions, identify potential biases, and confidently make business decisions based on data-driven insights.
